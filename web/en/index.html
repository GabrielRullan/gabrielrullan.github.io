Common Challenges

Critical bottlenecks on the desk of a CEO or CFO

Real operational friction that erodes margins and slows down decision-making in growing and mid-sized companies.

Conflicting numbers in executive meetings

Sales, Finance, and Operations present different revenue or margin figures. Leadership meetings are spent debating where each number came from instead of making strategic decisions.

Impact: Wasted executive time and high-stakes decisions made on shaky ground.

Heavy ERP investment, yet reporting lives in manual spreadsheets

Despite paying for an ERP or CRM, actual reporting relies on key staff spending hours every week copying, pasting, and reconciling fragile spreadsheets.

Impact: Operational vulnerability and critical key-person dependency risk.

Delayed monthly closes and rear-view mirror visibility

Consolidated performance figures arrive weeks after month-end. By the time a budget variance or margin drop is spotted, the financial impact is already irreversible.

Impact: Inability to course-correct commercial or operational deviations in real time.

The dilemma between massive consultancies or full-time hiring

Traditional consultancies charge steep retainers and staff junior generalists, while hiring a full-time internal data team takes months of recruiting and heavy fixed overhead.

Impact: Financial overspend, external bureaucracy, and zero knowledge transfer.
